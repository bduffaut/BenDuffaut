LLM judges like DeepEval, Ragas and Promptfoo score other models' answers. But who checks the judge?

mtlj changes ("mutates") answers in ways that *should* lower a score, like dropping a fact or
contradicting the question, and in ways that *shouldn't*, like rewording or padding. Then it uses
statistics to check whether the judge actually notices.

## My role

I'm the team's Scrum Master and own DevOps. I also build across the app.
