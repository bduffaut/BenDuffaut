Mode & Surface is a component library of 40+ Vue 3 components: app shells, navigation, overlays,
forms, tables, charts, kanban boards and more. It's credited to M&S Design and Development, and I'm
one of the contributors.

## What makes it different

- **Plain CSS, no provider.** Every component ships its own stylesheet in a cascade layer, so apps can override anything (including with Tailwind utilities) without fighting specificity.
- **Pay for what you import.** Import one component and you ship only its JS and CSS.
- **Copy what you want to own.** A CLI copies component source into your app when you'd rather own it.
- **Modes and density.** Light, dark or auto, plus comfortable and compact density, all set with data attributes.
