Upload a match photo or video, or point a webcam at a court, and TennisTracker runs five models over it.

## The models

| Module | What it does | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CNN classifier | Player, ball or background for each image crop | 99.8% val accuracy |
| Keypoint detector | 15 court landmarks, used to map positions onto a 2D court | 0.04 px avg val error |
| Faster R-CNN detector | Player and ball bounding boxes with confidence | F1 0.894 (P 0.879, R 0.910) |
| LSTM | Predicts the next ball position from the last 8 | ~12 px avg error at 1280×720 |
| VLM (Gemma via Ollama) | Answers natural-language questions about the scene | Pretrained |

An MLP baseline runs on the same crops so the CNN's gains can be measured, not assumed.

## The app

A Streamlit interface with image, video and camera modes. Video runs detection and tracking on
every third frame and plays back with bounding boxes and trajectory trails. A Court View tab maps
players and the ball onto a top-down court, and a Chat tab sends a representative frame to the VLM.
Any model can be retrained from inside the app, and the new weights hot-reload.

## Engineering

Every module has the same train and load interface, so models can be retrained and swapped
independently. The training code is written from scratch, including custom loss functions.
Device selection picks Apple Silicon (MPS), CUDA or CPU automatically. Coordinates stay
normalized to [0, 1] everywhere and are only converted to pixels at render time.
